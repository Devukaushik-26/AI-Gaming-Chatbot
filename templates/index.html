VALORANT MAPS & MECHANICS

Valorant features 13 standard maps (with a rotating 7-map competitive pool each Act, plus Team Deathmatch arenas and The Range). Every map rewards knowing its unique layout and mechanics.

3 SITES // DOORS

Lotus & Haven

Three-site maps that stretch defenses thin and punish slow rotations; Lotus adds rotating stone doors and a breakable wall.

MECHANIC

Summit

Three-lane, two-site academy map featuring droppable walls that can seal routes and reshape sightlines mid-round.

NO MID // TELEPORTERS

Bind

Two sites with no traditional mid lane and one-way teleporters for fast cross-map rotates and flanks.

CLASSIC // DOORS

Ascent & Sunset

Classic three-lane layouts where mid control matters heavily, with sealable site doors on Ascent and a mid mechanical door on Sunset.

VERTICAL & HIGH RISK

Abyss & Icebox

Abyss features open chasms with gravity knockouts, while Icebox packs verticality, ziplines, and layered angles.

MORE ROTATION MAPS

Split, Breeze, Fracture, Pearl & Corrode

Split uses ascender ropes and tight angles, Breeze favors long sightlines, Fracture splits attacker spawns, Pearl focuses on map geo, and Corrode centers on a radianite salt mine.

WEAPONS & ARSENAL

Choose weapons based on round budget, engagement distance, and your role in the round.

RIFLES // FULL BUY

Vandal & Phantom

Vandal deals lethal headshots at any range; Phantom offers faster fire rate, silenced shots, and smoother sprays.

BUDGET RIFLES

Bulldog & Guardian

Bulldog brings scope burst fire for mid-range value, while Guardian rewards accurate semi-auto one-taps.

SMGS

Spectre & Stinger

Great for anti-eco and half-buy rounds: Spectre is silenced and versatile, while Stinger shreds up close with high fire rate.

SIDEARMS

Classic, Ghost, Sheriff, Frenzy, Shorty & Bandit

Strong pistol-round and eco options—from the free Classic and silenced Ghost to heavy-hitting Sheriff and close-range sidearms.

SNIPERS

Marshal, Outlaw & Operator

Marshal is light and mobile, Outlaw packs twin-barrel punch, and Operator locks down long lanes with one-shot body damage.

SHOTGUNS & HEAVIES

Bucky, Judge, Ares & Odin

Bucky and Judge dominate tight corners, while Ares and Odin suppress chokes and penetrate cover.

ECONOMY & GAMEPLAY TIPS

Pistol & Eco Rounds

Pair a sidearm with shields or utility on pistol round, and save credits together on eco rounds so your team can full-buy next round.

Full Buy Priority

A true full buy balances rifle, shields, and key abilities—never buy a rifle with zero utility or shields if your team is saving.

Minimap & Rotations

Check the minimap often to spot enemy utility, call early rotates, and avoid leaving sites or flanks unguarded.

Gunfight Discipline

Keep crosshairs at head height, stop moving before shooting, and use smokes, flashes, or info before peeking.
